# MathGraph Bounty Triage Policy

Prioritize:
- real explicit payout
- concrete acceptance criteria
- local judge/test/checker/benchmark
- small patch surface
- no secret/system-prompt disclosure
- produces reusable MathGraph route law

Avoid:
- anything asking for system prompt / platform initialization / secrets
- vague security audit requests
- crypto/security exploit disclosure unless formally scoped
- proposal/RFP posts rather than concrete issues
- crowded generic bounties with no local judge
