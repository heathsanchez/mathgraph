+## Benchmark smoke check
+
+The benchmark suite can be smoke-tested locally with:
+
+    julia --project=benchmark benchmark/run_smoke.jl
+
+The smoke runner activates the benchmark project, instantiates dependencies, loads `benchmark/benchmarks.jl`, selects one small representative benchmark, and runs it with a tiny `BenchmarkTools` sample. It prints `QUANTUMSAVORY_BENCHMARK_SMOKE_OK` on success.
+
+A specific benchmark leaf can be selected with a slash-separated path, for example:
+
+    QUANTUMSAVORY_BENCHMARK_SMOKE_PATH=register/creation_and_initialization/from_tests julia --project=benchmark benchmark/run_smoke.jl
