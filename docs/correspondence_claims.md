# Correspondence Claims

Correspondence claims connect semantic conditions to syntactic axioms, motifs
to certificate families, roots to reasons, or obstruction signatures to blocked
continuations.

They are useful for discovery and explanation, especially when moving between
object logics, algebraic motifs, and certificate atlases.

They are advisory unless backed by explicit proof or countermodel artifacts with
safe trust/provenance. A verified-looking correspondence without an artifact is
not a terminal certificate.
