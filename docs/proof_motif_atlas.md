# Proof Motif Atlas

The proof motif atlas is the TRUE-side analogue of the finite-countermodel
motif/root work. It groups verified or imported TRUE-side implication rows by
repeated proof shape: variable identification, projection-forced proof,
transitivity chain, source collapse, target substitution, and similar motifs.

A proof motif is not a proof. It is a compressed observation that many proof
rows appear to share a route or shape. It can guide constructor pressure and
cut discovery, but it cannot promote a claim unless backed by a verified proof
artifact or certificate chain.

The atlas is intentionally lightweight and can be built from CSV/JSONL rows
without Lean installed.
