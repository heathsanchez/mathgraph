# Metalogic Labs / MathGraph - Verification Memory for AI

## Problem

AI systems generate reasoning faster than humans can audit.

## Product Thesis

Verification needs memory. MathGraph is a generative verification kernel that
turns claims into auditable outcomes and stores what survived.

## Current Proof Point

- 36 finite-checked countermodels
- +8.0 held-out gain
- zero trust-boundary violations

## Why Now

AI coding, AI math, AI science, and AI compliance all need trustworthy reasoning
infrastructure.

## Wedge

into developer verification APIs.
